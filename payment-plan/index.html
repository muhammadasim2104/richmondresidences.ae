Richmond Residences Al Furjan — Overview

45/5/50 Payment Structure

45%

During Construction

Forty-five percent of the purchase price is payable in instalments throughout the construction period, before handover of Tower 1.

5%

On Completion

Five percent is due upon completion and handover of your Richmond District apartment.

50%

Post-Handover (39 Months)

The remaining fifty percent is paid in instalments over 39 months after handover, giving owners time to occupy or rent the unit while completing payments.

Tower 1 handover is scheduled for Q1 2029. Starting prices: studios from AED 943,500, 1-bedroom from AED 1,871,700, 2-bedroom from AED 2,584,680. See the Richmond District price list for full pricing.
