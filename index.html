Project overview

Richmond District — A Branded Masterplan in Al Furjan

Richmond Residences, known officially as Richmond District, is a new integrated community in Al Furjan developed by Mira Developments. The masterplan brings together five residential buildings and one office tower, all linked by a shared podium offering retail, wellness, and leisure at ground level.

This marks Mira Developments' first residential partnership with John Richmond, the British fashion house established in London in the 1980s — and the first John Richmond-branded homes anywhere in Dubai. Tower 1, the opening residential phase, is now launched.

Mira Developments has built a track record of branded collaborations including Bentley Home, ETRO Home, ELIE SAAB, Jacob & Co., Trussardi, and Gianfranco Ferré Home.

Architecture & design

Mashrabiya Façades & John Richmond Interiors

Operable Mashrabiya Screens

The building exteriors feature traditional mashrabiya screens that residents can open and close. These elements add privacy, cut heat gain in summer, and still let daylight through — so the façade shifts in character as light changes through the day.

Move-In Ready by John Richmond

Each apartment arrives fully furnished with interiors conceived by John Richmond. Furniture, fixtures, materials, and finishes are all included — so owners receive a complete home at handover, not an empty shell.

Investment appeal

Why Consider Richmond Residences by Mira

Mira Care Warranty

Every Richmond District home includes Mira Care — described as the UAE's first five-year maintenance warranty. It covers core building systems, structural elements, MEP installations, lighting, and paintwork. Furniture carries a three-year warranty; fixtures are covered for five years.

45/5/50 Payment Plan

The 45/5/50 structure splits payments across construction (45%), completion (5%), and a 39-month post-handover period for the remaining 50%. This gives buyers time to settle the balance after taking possession. See the Richmond District payment plan breakdown.

Metro-Driven Rental Demand

Direct adjacency to Discovery Gardens Metro Station puts Richmond Residences Al Furjan within reach of major employment and leisure hubs across Dubai. Fully furnished, hotel-serviced apartments near a metro stop tend to attract strong tenant interest in established communities like Al Furjan.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Richmond Residences (Richmond District) by Mira Developments?

Richmond Residences is the marketing name for Richmond District, a new residential masterplan in Al Furjan, Dubai, developed by Mira Developments in partnership with British fashion house John Richmond. The project comprises five residential towers and one office building connected by a shared podium with retail, wellness, and leisure facilities. Tower 1 is the first residential release and is now available.

What apartment types are available at Richmond Residences Al Furjan?

Richmond Residences Al Furjan offers studios, 1-bedroom, and 2-bedroom apartments. Every home is fully furnished with interiors designed by John Richmond, including furniture, fixtures, materials, and finishes — delivered move-in ready at handover.

What are the prices at Richmond Residences, Al Furjan?

Studios start from AED 943,500 (USD 256,909) at 39 sqm / 420 sqft. 1-bedroom apartments start from AED 1,871,700 (USD 509,653) at 87.9 sqm / 946 sqft. 2-bedroom apartments start from AED 2,584,680 (USD 703,793) at 123 sqm / 1,322 sqft.

What is the payment plan for Richmond District?

Richmond District follows a 45/5/50 payment structure: 45% is payable during the construction period before handover, 5% is due upon completion, and the remaining 50% is spread across 39 months after handover.

When is the handover date for Richmond Residences?

Handover for Tower 1 at Richmond Residences is scheduled for Q1 2029. Tower 1 is the first residential tower released within the Richmond District masterplan.

Where is Richmond District located in Dubai?

Richmond District sits in Al Furjan, Dubai, directly next to Discovery Gardens Metro Station. From the site, Bluewaters Island is 8 minutes away, Palm Jumeirah 10 minutes, Downtown Dubai 18 minutes, and Al Maktoum International Airport 20 minutes.

What amenities does Richmond Residences Al Furjan offer?

Residents have access to three resort-style pools, decorative water features, a luxury lobby, spa and wellness facilities, a state-of-the-art gym, an outdoor fitness area, a padel court, a yoga zone, cafés, restaurants, and retail within the shared podium. Valet parking, concierge, housekeeping, cleaning, and in-residence dining are included as 5-star hotel-level services.

Who is the developer of Richmond District?

Richmond District is developed by Mira Developments, a Dubai-based developer (TRN 104070222500003, Trade License No. 1075912). This is Mira's first residential collaboration with John Richmond. Mira has previously partnered with Bentley Home, ETRO Home, ELIE SAAB, Jacob & Co., Trussardi, and Gianfranco Ferré Home.
