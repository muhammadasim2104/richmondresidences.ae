What We Know About Richmond District Tower 4 Today

Richmond District (also marketed as Richmond Residences) is developed by Mira Developments in Al Furjan, Dubai, in collaboration with John Richmond.

Richmond District Tower 4 is under construction with construction starting 20 April 2026. Sales opened 20 April 2026.

Richmond District Tower 4 comprises 51 units. Across the five towers currently tracked at Richmond District, there are 255 units in total (Business Tower, Towers 2–5).
